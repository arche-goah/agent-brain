Nothing between the pr-ball line and the shared-memory check reads either result, so both start at `pr-search` and are collected with `wait`: the shared-memory check prints at its old place (open-items still reads the requests at the fetched `origin/main`), the `PR waiting on you` line moves to right after the open items it qualifies. A failed `mktemp` falls back to the foreground run. Not cached on purpose: a whose-move line a day old would name a settled move.
