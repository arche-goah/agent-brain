- **`ci-watch.sh pr` reports a failed check at once instead of waiting for every other job.** (test: T1) Measured 2026-10-21 on a core PR: `lint` failed after about six minutes while one Windows portability job kept running for another eighteen; the watch said nothing the whole time, because a RED verdict waited for `pending == 0`, and the red stood unseen until the operator asked why the watcher was silent. A failed check never turns green, so a `fail` bucket with jobs still running now exits RED at once and names how many are still running (after the usual check that the PR head is its branch tip, so an older run's red is not reported). A `cancel` bucket still waits — a cancelled check can belong to a superseded run.
